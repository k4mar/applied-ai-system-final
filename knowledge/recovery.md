# Recovering when you fall behind

## When a win is no longer guaranteed
If the live interval holds more numbers than the remaining attempts can search
(more than 2 to the power of attempts left), a win is no longer guaranteed. The coach
says so honestly instead of promising a win it cannot deliver.

## The midpoint is still the best play when behind
Even when a guaranteed win is gone, guessing the midpoint gives the highest chance of
finding the secret, because it keeps the most options open for later attempts. Random
"hail mary" guesses lower the odds.

## Last attempt
With one attempt left, every number in the live interval is equally likely. Any number
inside the interval is a fair final guess; a number outside it is a guaranteed loss.

## After a wasted guess
A wasted guess (a repeat, or a number already ruled out) does not change the interval.
Recompute the midpoint of the same interval and continue; do not try to "make up" the lost
attempt with risky guesses.
